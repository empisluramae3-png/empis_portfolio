About Me

Designing with Clarity & Purpose

Luramae E. Empis

BSIT Student • Cebu Eastern College

Hello! I am Luramae Encarnacion Empis, a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) student at Cebu Eastern College.

My primary passion lies in User Interface (UI) Design and front-end development. I enjoy bridging the gap between functional systems and pleasing visuals, ensuring every element is easy on the eyes, accessible, and straightforward to navigate.

Through various academic and group projects—ranging from community management tools like the Barangay Mambaling web application to interactive games and canvas graphics—I continuously refine my wireframing, prototyping, and responsive web layouts.
